Nonprofit: forming in Wisconsin

Wisconsin Department of Financial Institutions. Low incorporation fees (around $35). Established nonprofit community. Confirm current charitable solicitation rules.

File Articles of Incorporation: File with Wisconsin DFI. Low fee. Online options available. Include IRS-required clauses.

Federal steps (every state)

  1. Get an EIN, free online at IRS.gov.
  2. Adopt bylaws and open a dedicated bank account.
  3. Apply for 501(c)(3) status: Form 1023-EZ ($275) or Form 1023 ($600) via Pay.gov.
  4. Register at SAM.gov for a UEI, then set up Grants.gov (allow 1–4 weeks).
  5. Keep up with state registration, annual reports and the Form 990 series.

Small business funding in Wisconsin

Solid SBA participation and regional lending options.

SBA programs are national. Start with SBA Loans and SBA Lender Match; a local SBDC can review your documents for free.

Questions

Where do I file to start a nonprofit in Wisconsin?

File with Wisconsin DFI. Low fee. Online options available. Include IRS-required clauses.

How much does it cost to apply for 501(c)(3) status?

IRS Form 1023-EZ costs $275 and the full Form 1023 costs $600, paid through Pay.gov. The EIN itself is free at IRS.gov.

Where can a small business in Wisconsin get funding help?

Solid SBA participation and regional lending options. SBA programs are national; SBA Lender Match (sba.gov/lendermatch) connects you with participating lenders.
