Start a nonprofit or fund a small business in Florida

The Florida filing office, fees and official links, plus the federal steps every nonprofit and small business follows. Information as of October 2026.

Nonprofit: forming in Florida

Florida Division of Corporations. Articles of Incorporation fee typically $35–$70 range depending on options. Online filing available. Check current charitable registration requirements.

File Articles of Incorporation: File with Florida Division of Corporations. Confirm current fee on official site. Online filing supported. Include required purpose language for 501(c)(3).

Federal steps (every state)

  1. Get an EIN, free online at IRS.gov.
  2. Adopt bylaws and open a dedicated bank account.
  3. Apply for 501(c)(3) status: Form 1023-EZ ($275) or Form 1023 ($600) via Pay.gov.
  4. Register at SAM.gov for a UEI, then set up Grants.gov (allow 1–4 weeks).
  5. Keep up with state registration, annual reports and the Form 990 series.

Small business funding in Florida

High SBA activity, especially in tourism, hospitality, and construction. Multiple district offices.

SBA programs are national. Start with SBA Loans and SBA Lender Match; a local SBDC can review your documents for free.

Questions

Where do I file to start a nonprofit in Florida?

File with Florida Division of Corporations. Confirm current fee on official site. Online filing supported. Include required purpose language for 501(c)(3).

How much does it cost to apply for 501(c)(3) status?

IRS Form 1023-EZ costs $275 and the full Form 1023 costs $600, paid through Pay.gov. The EIN itself is free at IRS.gov.

Where can a small business in Florida get funding help?

High SBA activity, especially in tourism, hospitality, and construction. Multiple district offices. SBA programs are national; SBA Lender Match (sba.gov/lendermatch) connects you with participating lenders.
