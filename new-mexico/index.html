Nonprofit: forming in New Mexico

New Mexico Secretary of State. Articles of Incorporation fee $25 (plus any payment processing charges). Online filing required via the Enterprise portal. Minimum 3 directors. Charitable solicitation registration with the Attorney General is typically required (often $0 fee). File Initial Corporate Report within 30 days of incorporation.

File Articles of Incorporation: File Nonprofit Corporation Articles of Incorporation with New Mexico Secretary of State through the online Enterprise portal. Fee $25. Include IRS-required purpose and dissolution language. Processing often 5–10 business days. Complete Initial Corporate Report (fee ~$10) within 30 days of receiving the certificate.

Federal steps (every state)

  1. Get an EIN, free online at IRS.gov.
  2. Adopt bylaws and open a dedicated bank account.
  3. Apply for 501(c)(3) status: Form 1023-EZ ($275) or Form 1023 ($600) via Pay.gov.
  4. Register at SAM.gov for a UEI, then set up Grants.gov (allow 1–4 weeks).
  5. Keep up with state registration, annual reports and the Form 990 series.

Small business funding in New Mexico

SBA district office serves the state. Regional CDFI and microloan options available. Confirm local business licensing and any industry-specific permits.

SBA programs are national. Start with SBA Loans and SBA Lender Match; a local SBDC can review your documents for free.

Questions

Where do I file to start a nonprofit in New Mexico?

File Nonprofit Corporation Articles of Incorporation with New Mexico Secretary of State through the online Enterprise portal. Fee $25. Include IRS-required purpose and dissolution language. Processing often 5–10 business days. Complete Initial Corporate Report (fee ~$10) within 30 days of receiving the certificate.

How much does it cost to apply for 501(c)(3) status?

IRS Form 1023-EZ costs $275 and the full Form 1023 costs $600, paid through Pay.gov. The EIN itself is free at IRS.gov.

Where can a small business in New Mexico get funding help?

SBA district office serves the state. Regional CDFI and microloan options available. Confirm local business licensing and any industry-specific permits. SBA programs are national; SBA Lender Match (sba.gov/lendermatch) connects you with participating lenders.
