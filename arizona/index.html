Start a nonprofit or fund a small business in Arizona

The Arizona filing office, fees and official links, plus the federal steps every nonprofit and small business follows. Information as of October 2026.

Nonprofit: forming in Arizona

Arizona Corporation Commission (ACC). Articles of Incorporation (Form C011) fee typically $40. Maricopa and Pima counties exempt from newspaper publication. No general charitable solicitation registration required. Minimum 1 director allowed (recommend 3+).

File Articles of Incorporation: File Form C011 with Arizona Corporation Commission (ACC) plus Certificate of Disclosure and Statutory Agent Acceptance. Standard fee ~$40. Include IRS purpose and dissolution language. Online filing available. Maricopa/Pima often exempt from publication.

Federal steps (every state)

  1. Get an EIN, free online at IRS.gov.
  2. Adopt bylaws and open a dedicated bank account.
  3. Apply for 501(c)(3) status: Form 1023-EZ ($275) or Form 1023 ($600) via Pay.gov.
  4. Register at SAM.gov for a UEI, then set up Grants.gov (allow 1–4 weeks).
  5. Keep up with state registration, annual reports and the Form 990 series.

Small business funding in Arizona

Arizona has the Microbusiness Loan Program ($2,000–$50,000 for businesses with ≤5 employees) via Office of Economic Opportunity and CDFI partners. Strong SBDC network in Maricopa area.

SBA programs are national. Start with SBA Loans and SBA Lender Match; a local SBDC can review your documents for free.

Questions

Where do I file to start a nonprofit in Arizona?

File Form C011 with Arizona Corporation Commission (ACC) plus Certificate of Disclosure and Statutory Agent Acceptance. Standard fee ~$40. Include IRS purpose and dissolution language. Online filing available. Maricopa/Pima often exempt from publication.

How much does it cost to apply for 501(c)(3) status?

IRS Form 1023-EZ costs $275 and the full Form 1023 costs $600, paid through Pay.gov. The EIN itself is free at IRS.gov.

Where can a small business in Arizona get funding help?

Arizona has the Microbusiness Loan Program ($2,000–$50,000 for businesses with ≤5 employees) via Office of Economic Opportunity and CDFI partners. Strong SBDC network in Maricopa area. SBA programs are national; SBA Lender Match (sba.gov/lendermatch) connects you with participating lenders.
