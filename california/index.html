Nonprofit: forming in California

California Secretary of State. Articles of Incorporation (Form ARTS-PB-501(c)(3)) fee $30. Must register with Attorney General Registry of Charitable Trusts (Form CT-1, typically $25–$50) within 30 days of receiving charitable assets. State tax exemption via Franchise Tax Board after IRS determination. Minimum 1 director allowed.

File Articles of Incorporation: File Form ARTS-PB-501(c)(3) with California Secretary of State via bizfile Online. Fee $30. Include IRS purpose and dissolution language. Online processing often 3–5 business days. Register for charitable solicitation promptly.

Federal steps (every state)

  1. Get an EIN, free online at IRS.gov.
  2. Adopt bylaws and open a dedicated bank account.
  3. Apply for 501(c)(3) status: Form 1023-EZ ($275) or Form 1023 ($600) via Pay.gov.
  4. Register at SAM.gov for a UEI, then set up Grants.gov (allow 1–4 weeks).
  5. Keep up with state registration, annual reports and the Form 990 series.

Small business funding in California

High volume of SBA lending and CDFI activity. Multiple district offices. Strong ecosystem but competitive. Check CalGold for local licensing.

SBA programs are national. Start with SBA Loans and SBA Lender Match; a local SBDC can review your documents for free.

Questions

Where do I file to start a nonprofit in California?

File Form ARTS-PB-501(c)(3) with California Secretary of State via bizfile Online. Fee $30. Include IRS purpose and dissolution language. Online processing often 3–5 business days. Register for charitable solicitation promptly.

How much does it cost to apply for 501(c)(3) status?

IRS Form 1023-EZ costs $275 and the full Form 1023 costs $600, paid through Pay.gov. The EIN itself is free at IRS.gov.

Where can a small business in California get funding help?

High volume of SBA lending and CDFI activity. Multiple district offices. Strong ecosystem but competitive. Check CalGold for local licensing. SBA programs are national; SBA Lender Match (sba.gov/lendermatch) connects you with participating lenders.
