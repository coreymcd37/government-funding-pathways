Nonprofit: forming in Kentucky

Kentucky Secretary of State. Articles of Incorporation fee $8 (one of the lowest). Must also record with county clerk within 30 days (additional local fee). Minimum 3 directors. Charitable solicitation registration with Attorney General ($0 fee).

File Articles of Incorporation: File Form NAI with Kentucky Secretary of State ($8). Online available. Within 30 days, record copy with county clerk in registered office county. Include 501(c)(3) purpose and dissolution language.

Federal steps (every state)

  1. Get an EIN, free online at IRS.gov.
  2. Adopt bylaws and open a dedicated bank account.
  3. Apply for 501(c)(3) status: Form 1023-EZ ($275) or Form 1023 ($600) via Pay.gov.
  4. Register at SAM.gov for a UEI, then set up Grants.gov (allow 1–4 weeks).
  5. Keep up with state registration, annual reports and the Form 990 series.

Small business funding in Kentucky

Low formation barriers. Access SBA and regional CDFIs.

SBA programs are national. Start with SBA Loans and SBA Lender Match; a local SBDC can review your documents for free.

Questions

Where do I file to start a nonprofit in Kentucky?

File Form NAI with Kentucky Secretary of State ($8). Online available. Within 30 days, record copy with county clerk in registered office county. Include 501(c)(3) purpose and dissolution language.

How much does it cost to apply for 501(c)(3) status?

IRS Form 1023-EZ costs $275 and the full Form 1023 costs $600, paid through Pay.gov. The EIN itself is free at IRS.gov.

Where can a small business in Kentucky get funding help?

Low formation barriers. Access SBA and regional CDFIs. SBA programs are national; SBA Lender Match (sba.gov/lendermatch) connects you with participating lenders.
